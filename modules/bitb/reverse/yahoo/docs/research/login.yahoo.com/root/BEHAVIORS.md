# Behaviors

## Global
- **Responsive changes:** The header switches from transparent background to white background on mobile. The logo changes from white text to purple text. The page background changes from the ad/promotional graphic to a plain background on mobile, and the login card centers instead of being right-aligned.

## Interaction Model
- Mostly click-driven.
- Focus states on the input field highlight the border in blue (`#0f69ff`).
- Buttons have hover states (`#7e1fff` to `#6001d2` for primary, `#ffffff` to `#f0f3f5` for secondary).
